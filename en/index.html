Bursa, November 2026

Dear guest,

We opened with my mother’s single door in Heykel; today we have three, but the pot is the same. Here are our four promises to you:

  1. 01

    Three doors, one recipe book.

    The bean stew simmering in Heykel is cooked to the same measure in Nilufer and Mudanya.

  2. 02

    Vegetables from the morning market.

    We do not write out-of-season vegetables on the board; that is why the calendar exists.

  3. 03

    Ask and we will tell you.

    Our cook will tell you what is in each dish; as everything is made in one kitchen, traces may be present.

  4. 04

    Finished dishes come off the board.

    We never reheat a pot that ran out and sell it the next day.

Nermin Arslan

Founder, Üç Kapı Lokantası (fictional)
